About me

AI product marketing manager, building AI systems that drive revenue for businesses.

Lagos, NigeriaRemote, worldwide

I help good products get chosen. That means knowing the customer better than the competition does, saying one clear thing, and building the system that proves it.

Over the last five years I've led go-to-market, positioning and market entry for technology companies with teams across the US, UK, Europe, Africa and Asia: a feature launch that brought in 5,000+ sign-ups in 48 hours with about 46% paying, a year leading market expansion for an AI platform that grew adoption 45% in a quarter, and a creator platform that went from under 50 users to nearly 600 in two weeks.

In 2025 I founded Creataws. I wanted to know whether I could take a product all the way from a written spec to paying users, so I built Opptiq myself with AI tools, priced it and launched it. With no ads, just two organic LinkedIn posts (one video, one written), it reached 260+ users in under 3 weeks, with paying customers from the first week. That experience changed how I lead: I now make product, pricing and go-to-market decisions with an owner's view of the trade-offs.

Today I'm looking for my next AI product marketing role: a team where I own how a product reaches its market, and answer for the numbers.
