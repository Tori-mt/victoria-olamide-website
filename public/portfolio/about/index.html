About me

Lawyer by training. Product marketer by practice. Founder by choice.

Lagos, NigeriaRemote, worldwide

I help good products get chosen. That means knowing the customer better than the competition does, saying one clear thing, and building the system that proves it.

I studied law at Obafemi Awolowo University and was called to the Nigerian Bar. Law taught me to build an argument from evidence, to anticipate objections and to make a decision I can defend. It turns out that is most of product marketing.

Over the last five years I've led go-to-market, positioning and market entry for technology companies in the United States, Germany and Nigeria: a feature launch that brought in 5,000+ sign-ups in 48 hours with about 46% paying, a year leading market expansion for an AI platform that grew adoption 45% in a quarter, and a creator platform that went from under 50 users to nearly 600 in two weeks.

In 2025 I founded Creataws. I wanted to know whether I could take a product all the way from a written spec to paying users, so I built Opptiq myself with AI tools, priced it, launched it and grew it to 260+ users in three weeks. That experience changed how I lead: I now make product, pricing and go-to-market decisions with an owner's view of the trade-offs.

Today I'm looking for my next leadership seat: a team where I own the decisions about how a product reaches its market, and answer for the numbers.
