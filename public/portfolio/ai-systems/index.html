FAQ

Before you book.

What business problems can an AI agent solve?

Anything repetitive, rules-based and time-sensitive: qualifying leads, answering customer questions, answering calls, booking appointments, reporting and internal admin. If your team does it the same way fifty times a week, an agent can probably help.

Will it replace my team?

No. It takes the repetitive work off your team so they can focus on the conversations and decisions that need a person. Anything sensitive is handed straight to a human.

Do I need to change the tools I use?

No. I connect the agent to the tools you already use, wherever they have an API or integration.

How long does it take?

A focused first agent usually goes from strategy call to live in a few weeks, depending on the integrations. We agree the scope and timeline on the call.

What happens on the strategy call?

Thirty minutes on your business: where work gets stuck, what an agent should handle first and what success looks like. You leave with a clear recommendation, whether or not we work together.
