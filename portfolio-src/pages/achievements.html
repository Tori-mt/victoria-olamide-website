I'll Tip · United States · Product Marketing Execution Partner (Contract) · Mar 2026 to present

I'll Tip

A network-and-hire platform for professionals, launching a new product feature.

Objective

Launch a new feature, turn attention into sign-ups quickly, and convert those sign-ups into paying customers.

My role

Led the go-to-market campaign and rollout: product positioning, launch messaging, campaign strategy and execution.

One clear message about what the feature does for the user, with every channel pointing to the same next step, run as a short, tightly managed execution sprint.

5,000+ sign-ups in 48 hours and about 46% paid conversion. I wrote the SOP behind the launch; the founder made it the standard process for the team, and I turned the results into a repeatable GTM playbook.

C · Founder results

My own product, my own numbers.

Opptiq by Creataws · Founder & product lead · Launched 17 Oct 2026

Opptiq

An AI opportunity intelligence platform that shows people where their expertise meets AI, backed by live job-market evidence.

Objective

Take a product from a written spec to a live, paid product with real users, without a funded team.

My role

Everything a founder owns: the PRD, positioning, pricing, the build itself with AI tools, payments, launch and growth.

Strategy & execution

A clear "AI for the work you already do" position; a free tier with two analyses to drive trial; a live cohort and community as launch channels; email onboarding and a job-application tracker to drive retention.

What changed

260+ users in under 3 weeks from two organic LinkedIn posts (one video, one written) and no paid ads, with paying customers from the first week. Since then: 180+ AI career analyses and 130+ personalised action plans.
