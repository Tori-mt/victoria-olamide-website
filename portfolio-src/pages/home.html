My approach

Decide first. Then build.

I trained as a lawyer, so I start where a good case starts: with evidence. Who is the customer, what do they already believe, and what would make them act today? I don't write a message until I can answer that.

Then I make the call. One position, one promise, one next step for every channel. I build the system that delivers it, from launch plans and SOPs to AI workflows and full products, and I measure until the number moves.
