What is Opptiq?
Opptiq is your AI career guide. You tell us about your work, and in minutes you get a personal report showing where AI fits into your skills, the problems you could solve, new ways to earn in dollars globally, and real remote jobs paying thousands of dollars for those skills right now.

Then Opptiq finds those jobs for you, tailors your CV, cover letter and portfolio to each one, and tracks every application, with a 60-day plan of small daily tasks so you lock in and land your dream role.
